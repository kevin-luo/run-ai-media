# X recommendation notes

Last verified: 2026-07-28.

Use official documentation as the source of truth. Re-check it when X changes its recommendation documentation or when an account strategy is revised.

## What X publicly confirms

X says recommendations use many signals and no single signal has a permanently fixed importance. Publicly described signals include:

- accounts and topics a person follows;
- likes, reposts, replies, quotes, and shares;
- watched media and video views;
- dwell time, post clicks, photo expands, profile clicks, and link opens;
- prior interaction between a reader and an author;
- topic and network popularity;
- negative feedback such as dislikes, mutes, blocks, dismissals, and reports;
- post features including language, length, media, URLs, duplication, and recency.

Trends emphasize conversations gaining popularity now, not topics that have merely been large for a long time.

## Operational implications

These are editorial choices informed by the public signals, not guaranteed ranking formulas:

- Give readers something worth pausing on: a native video, screenshot, concrete detail, or unresolved practical question.
- Earn profile clicks with a clear account identity and a post that demonstrates what the account regularly knows or builds.
- Join conversations where the account has genuine topic fit; repeated author-reader interactions can help relevance.
- Prefer specific replies that invite a real answer over generic praise or engagement bait.
- Avoid duplicated copy, repeated CTAs, reply bursts, and several near-identical posts.
- Let a post accumulate conversation. Do not bury it immediately under another original post.
- Watch negative feedback and silence. More output is not a fix for weak relevance.

## Account rules for @luosilent

- Publish no more than one normal original post per 24 hours.
- Allow a second original only for an unusually strong time-sensitive topic, at least six hours after the previous original.
- Space ordinary replies by at least 90 minutes.
- Put outbound links in the first self-reply, not the main post.
- Use at most one hashtag, only when it is a natural topic label people are actively using.
- Never trade accuracy or voice for a supposed algorithm trick.
