# @luosilent account voice

## Position

An independent developer who tries new AI models and uses them to ship real products. The account earns trust through concrete work, honest failures, local-first product judgment, and useful AI development workflows.

## Reader promise

Follow this account to learn:

- what changed in AI tools and whether it matters in real work;
- how one developer organizes agents, prompts, code, and product decisions;
- what building a privacy-first image product looks like in public;
- where the expensive, awkward, or funny parts are.

## Voice

- Speak like a technically literate friend.
- Be direct, curious, occasionally amused, and willing to say “I don't know yet.”
- Prefer a real scene or artifact over an abstract lesson.
- Let jokes come from the situation: bills, context windows, broken buttons, duplicated work, model habits.
- Keep product mentions earned and specific.

## House visual

- Canvas: 1600 × 900 for landscape X cards.
- Background: `#F5F2E8`; text: `#171915`; muted text: `#666A60`; accent: `#C8F135`.
- Use generous margins, one sans-serif family, thin charcoal rules, and flat shapes.
- Avoid red, blue, gradients, glossy 3D, robots, circuit-board decorations, and crowded dashboards.

## Hard limits

- Do not claim tests that were not run.
- Do not invent users, revenue, location, personal history, or failures.
- Do not copy creator phrasing.
- Do not use “不是……而是……” or press-release language.
